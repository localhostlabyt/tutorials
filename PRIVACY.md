# Privacy policy: localhost.LAB uploader

localhost.LAB uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the
[localhost.LAB](https://www.youtube.com/@localhostlabyt) YouTube channel.
It uploads videos, thumbnails and captions to that channel, manages its playlists,
and reads the channel's own YouTube Analytics.

- It is not offered to anyone else, and no other Google accounts sign in to it.
- It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other users.
- The access token stays on the channel owner's own server and is used only by this tool.
- Access can be revoked at any time in the Google account settings
  (https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Questions: leave a comment on the localhost.LAB YouTube channel.
